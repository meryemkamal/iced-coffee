Iced coffee

Welcome to our iced coffee page! ☕ Discover a refreshing and delicious iced coffee recipe, perfect for hot summer days. 🧊 Follow our easy recipe and enjoy!

Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoons of instant coffee
  • 1 cup of cold milk
  • 1 tablespoon of sugar
  • Ice cubes
  • Chocolate syrup (optional)

Instructions

  1. Add the instant coffee and sugar to a glass.
  2. Pour in a little hot water and mix well.
  3. Add the ice cubes.
  4. Pour in the cold milk.
  5. Stir well and enjoy your refreshing iced coffee! ☕
